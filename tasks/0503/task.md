## Task Name
Pivot Table with Interactive Visualization

#### Task Description
Your company's leadership team requires a three-dimensional view of the workforce. They don't just want a list; they need to see financials, trends, and logistics simultaneously. To avoid a cluttered report, you must build three independent Pivot Tables, each feeding a specific visualization.
This task tests your ability to manipulate the same dataset in three different ways to answer distinct business questions:
- How much do we pay each department on average?
- How many people were hired each month in a US office location?
- What is our ratio of contract vs. full-time staff in each office?

#### Learning Objectives
- Organize cells into Tables with Headers.
- Create multiple pivots from one source table without them interfering with each other.
- Learn to group specific dates (Hire Dates) into broader months or quarters for trend analysis.
- Use "Show Values As % of Row Total" to compare workforce compositions across offices of different sizes.
- sChoosing the right chart type (Bar vs. Line vs. Stacked) for the specific data type.

#### Final Outcome
An Excel workforce report with three Pivot Tables and matching charts that show department pay, monthly hiring trends, and staff type composition by office.
