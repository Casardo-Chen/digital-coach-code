## Task Name
Calculate Conditional Revenue with SUMIFS in Excel

#### Task Description
Using a raw sales dataset, you need to answer "What was the total revenue for 'Furniture' in the 'South' region for the month of January?" You will use SUMIFS to handle multiple overlapping conditions simultaneously. 

#### Learning Objectives
- Construct a basic table in Excel and use manual filters to subset the data.
- Apply multiple filters automatically by constructing a SUMIFS formula that targets up to three helper columns.
- Explain why isolating variables into separate columns improves data "auditability."

#### Final Outcome
An Excel table with a working SUMIFS formula that returns the total January revenue for Furniture sales in the South region.
